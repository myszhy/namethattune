Name That Tune

A music quiz for iPhone that plays real songs from Apple Music.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email lesniewskakj@gmail.com.

Common questions

Do I need Apple Music? You need to allow the app to use Apple Music. With an Apple Music subscription you hear full songs; without one, the game plays 30-second previews.

Where are my scores and decks saved? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.
