Privacy Policy

Name That Tune · Last updated 10 October 2026

Name That Tune does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

The name you type to play, your scores and rankings, and your flashcard decks are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Apple Music

The app asks for permission to use Apple Music so it can find and play songs. It does not read your library, playlists or listening history, and nothing from your Apple Music account leaves your device.

MusicBrainz

To show facts about an artist on a flashcard, the app sends the artist's name to MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org), a free public music database. No information about you is included. MusicBrainz's own privacy policy is at metabrainz.org/privacy.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
